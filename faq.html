FAQ

Quick answers about Quorbit.

Is Quorbit free?

Yes. Daily is free forever. Optional passes are coming soon; see Pricing.

How do we pick and add questions?

Questions are sourced from reputable references and curated mining. AI-assisted search and a semantic similarity index flag near-duplicates and repeats, so the Daily never repeats a question, answer or concept. Every question is then read and edited by a human for accuracy, zero ambiguity and fair options, and pictures are checked for rights and credit.

Can I contribute questions?

Yes, please. We welcome questions from quizmasters and quiz enthusiasts, now and in future, via the Submit a question form in the app or info@iquorbit.com. By submitting you confirm it is your original work or that you have the rights to share it; we may edit, reword, schedule or decline it at our discretion; if accepted we credit you by a handle (optional); there is no payment unless agreed in writing; and you grant Quorbit a non-exclusive licence to use it, as set out in the Terms.

Where is my progress saved?

On this device, in your browser. Optional sign-in to sync across devices is coming later.

I found a mistake or a bug

Thank you! Write to support@iquorbit.com with your device, browser and a screenshot.
