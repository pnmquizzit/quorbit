Quorbit site map — layers & access

How the whole GH Pages app is reached: shell chrome → primary tabs → ☰ sheet → screen flows → paywall / owner gates → local data & banks. Hash routes as of Oct 2026 (v1.4).

Five access layers. Quorbit is a single-page hash app. The reader never leaves index.html; every screen is #/…. Primary reach is the bottom tab bar; secondary reach is the ☰ sheet; deep links and query flags unlock demos and the owner desk.

10. Content & bank layer

  • Daily — curated 7-round mix (Rapid + Cricket slots, Hard skew).
  • Topics / packs — freemium window; packs gated by topic free left.
  • Quorbit Total — endless Hard-skew; scoped by unlock tier.
  • Community — submit → pending → desk → approved + credit + firewall.
  • Visual — Commons pic MCQs + Hard CONNECT (Lapu-Lapu style).
  • Taxonomy — Topic → subtopic → leaf (quorbit_subtopic_tree_v1.md).
