Refund & cancellation

Daily is free, so most players never pay anything. For future paid passes, our policy is simple and fair.

Digital passes

Quorbit passes are digital and start working as soon as they activate. Passes are not on sale yet; this policy applies once they are.

When you get a full refund

If a payment failed but money left your account, if you were charged twice for the same pass, or if a pass you paid for does not activate.

How to ask

Email support@iquorbit.com within 7 days of the payment, with the date, amount and payment reference (a screenshot is fine). We usually reply within 48 hours.

Cancellation

Passes are one-time and do not auto-renew, so there is nothing to cancel. If a renewing plan is ever offered, you will be able to cancel anytime before the next renewal.

Refund timing

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days depending on your bank.
